Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

About the application

WildBond Calendar Sync is a personal tool designed to synchronize selected Google Calendar scheduling information with a private iCloud calendar.

Information accessed

The application accesses Google Calendar event information authorized by the account holder, including event dates, times, and creator information needed to identify relevant events.

How information is used

Calendar information is processed exclusively to determine and synchronize the relevant scheduling intervals to the user's designated iCloud calendar.

Data sharing

The application is not offered as a public service. Calendar information is transferred to the user's authorized iCloud calendar for synchronization. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Data storage and security

Authentication credentials are configured as private secrets in the application's execution environment. The application does not intentionally publish calendar event information on this website.

Data retention

Calendar events created in iCloud remain there until updated or removed through the synchronization process or by the account holder.

Contact

For questions about this application and its data handling, contact the application owner through the contact details provided on the Google OAuth consent screen.
